# element14 Project14 "Make a Connection" — competition rules

Verified on **5 September 2026** against the official [competition page][page] and the official nine-page [2026 Terms and Conditions PDF][terms]. This is a project-planning summary, not a substitute for the current official terms.

## Submission gate

- **Deadline:** 23:59 UK time on Sunday, **13 September 2026**. Both sources agree.
- **Entry format:** submit electronically on element14 Community as a project article/blog. The competition page says one project blog covering the whole project is required.
- **Required content:** give a step-by-step build description or instructions; explain how the project fits the theme of electronics that send a message or signal; show a complete, functional project. The page explicitly asks for **a video and photographs**. The T&Cs say video and/or photos/images, so use the stricter page requirement and include both.
- **Human submission required:** T&Cs §2.6 says an application may not be submitted by an agent. Prepare and review the blog in advance, but the entrant must log in and submit it personally.
- **Profile:** the entrant's element14 Community profile must have an accurate, current first name, last name, address, and phone number (T&Cs §3.5).

## Eligibility and originality

- Entrant must be a registered element14 Community member and at least 18. Natural or legal persons, firms, companies, and groups/unincorporated bodies may enter, except Avnet/Premier Farnell employees and affiliates; organiser staff/direct families; judges/direct families; and residents or nationals of countries subject to US, EU, or UK sanctions, embargoes, or trade restrictions.
- Entry must not breach applicable law, contracts, employer policy, or require the organiser to obtain a licence/authorisation to deal with the entrant. The organiser can demand prompt evidence of compliance.
- The project can be in progress, previously created, or not previously posted. A newly invented project is **not** required by the page. Multiple applications are allowed only when each is a unique project.
- The entrant warrants that they own all IP used in the submission. Do not publish client data, proprietary material, unlicensed media, or other third-party assets without appropriate rights and attribution.

## Judging and supporting material

- A judgeable entry must be a **completed project**. Originality, innovation, and technical merit are equally weighted. element14 staff judges select winners at their discretion; their decision is final.
- The page asks entrants to demonstrate the complete and functional project and says high-quality images/video are appreciated. T&Cs strongly encourage legible images of at least **640×480 pixels at 72 dpi**, relevant software code, applicable 3D/CNC files, and a component list including manufacturer part numbers.
- Source code is **encouraged when applicable**, not stated as mandatory, public, or required to be hosted on GitHub. Repository publication is therefore a separate MAZ Pocket release decision, still subject to its secret/public-release gate.

## IP, publicity, and disqualification

- The participant keeps ownership of submitted IP. By entering, they authorise the organiser to copy, reproduce, publish, and use the submission and license its IP for the contest or its promotion. They also grant use of their likeness, photos, logos, trademarks, audio, and video for those purposes and agree to participate positively in publicity.
- Incomplete, corrupted, incomprehensible, late, or non-electronic applications are not considered. Entries must not infringe third-party IP or include illegal, immoral, discriminatory, offensive, socially taboo, or site-policy-breaching material.
- The organiser may disqualify rule breaches, disruptive/abusive conduct, or conduct likely to harm the organiser/contest/judges' reputation. It may amend the terms, cancel/suspend the contest, or decline to award a prize.

## Prize-region points

- The page says the organiser can use global stock, include a plug adaptor, or find a local equivalent if a prize is unavailable or unsuitable in the winner's region. The T&Cs allow substitution and make the winner responsible for taxes, duties, and other charges.
- Sanctioned-country exclusions above remain the principal geographic eligibility restriction stated in the T&Cs. The entrant must self-confirm age, residence/nationality, employment-policy, and profile-detail eligibility before submission.

## Engineering and publication consequences

1. Make the connection chain explicit in the blog and demonstrate it on the physical Cardputer with both video and still photographs; automated tests alone cannot satisfy the functional-project evidence requirement.
2. Capture step-by-step build evidence, a readable architecture/connection diagram, a component list, and the relevant code/configuration instructions while implementing.
3. Present MAZ Pocket as one completed, reliable project. Existing work is eligible, but the entry should make its originality, innovation, and technical merit easy to judge.
4. Use only sanitized demonstration content and cleared visual/audio assets. Preserve third-party notices and state limitations honestly.
5. Have the entrant perform the final logged-in submission before the deadline and retain the published URL and timestamp as evidence.
