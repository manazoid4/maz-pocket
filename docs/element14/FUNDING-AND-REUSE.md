# Competition reuse and Kickstarter track

## Reusable asset pack

Create each asset once and keep an editable source plus a sanitized export:

- one-sentence product claim and longer project description;
- connection-chain diagram and component list;
- clean repository/release link and reproducible install steps;
- physical proof ledger with commit, route, date and three-run results;
- device, human interaction and PC-result photographs;
- short demonstration master video plus modular clips for CALL, CAPTURE, AGENTS and CONTROL;
- origin story, build decisions, failures, fixes and measured limitations;
- BOM, prototype cost and known manufacturing assumptions;
- licensing, attribution, privacy and secret-scan record;
- press kit with sanitized screenshots, captions and founder bio.

For each future competition, verify current rules and create a thin entry-specific layer: eligibility, deadline/timezone, theme fit, judging weights, required media, submission owner and proof threshold. Do not change the product merely to accumulate competition features.

## Kickstarter readiness after element14

Treat the competition build as evidence for a later campaign, not as a production promise. A campaign is ready only after:

1. the physical connection loop is repeatable and filmed;
2. the offer is defined: firmware/software package, supported hardware, included services and exclusions;
3. a sanitized client use case shows practical value beyond a maker demo;
4. BOM, packaging, fulfilment, support, taxes and contingency are costed;
5. manufacturing and supply assumptions have named evidence owners;
6. privacy, licences, trademarks and campaign claims are reviewed;
7. a pre-launch page and audience plan exist;
8. reward quantities and delivery dates follow proven capacity.

The first commercial story is: **capture a thought on a pocket device and turn it into a useful, bounded action on the PC**. Use synthetic client data in public demonstrations. Keep broader desktop control, custom hardware and production commitments outside the element14 sprint.

## Model workflow template

- Sol: source collection, CLI evidence, deterministic checks, cleanup, asset production and routine implementation.
- Astra: architecture selection, security/execution boundaries, cross-cutting reliability decisions and final skeptical release review.
- Human: physical filming judgement, legal/eligibility confirmation, commercial promises, pricing and final platform submission.
